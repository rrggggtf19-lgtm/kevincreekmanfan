KEEP SHOWING UP

The beginning

Make the

English Muscle began with a simple question: what if getting stronger did not have to feel serious all the time? The answer became a daily practice of training hard, laughing at the misses and sharing the honest bits in between.

Today, the handle is a place for people who want more from their sessions and less noise around them. No perfect routines. No performance of perfection. Just showing up again.

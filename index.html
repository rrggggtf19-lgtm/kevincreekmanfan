The person behind the work

Meet
Kevin.

Kevin Creekman is a tattoo artist, model and social media creator known for his large-scale custom tattoos and remarkable body transformation. His story is one of reinvention: after losing more than half his body weight, he used tattoos to cover scars and turn a difficult chapter into an unmistakable visual identity.

Alongside tattooing and content creation, Kevin has worked as a musician, actor, personal trainer, blogger and social worker. Today, his work brings those experiences together through honest stories, transformation and art.
